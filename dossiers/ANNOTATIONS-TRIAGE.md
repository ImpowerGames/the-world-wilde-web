## 1.5 Identification keys established from the mirrored index/footnotes (finding-only)

- **"Sir John" = I. D. W. Ashton.** Editors' footnote at printed 979n (mirror, folio-bearing): a visiting-card "Mr I. D. W. Ashton. Piccadilly Club, W." — "Further than that he has not been traced. Meyerfeld says that he was nicknamed Sir John," corroborated by "references to him and St Malo on pp. 1118–9. See also p. 1067." So the blue 'Sir John' excerpts (ann. 1304–1315) = Ashton, an untraced Naples-1897 companion, introduced by Wilde to Turner (printed 979). Export B also has him by name: "Dear good Ashton is here" (3490), "Ashton seems quite devoted to the Sphinx" (3494). The owner may not have connected 'Sir John' and 'Ashton'.
- **"To the Sphinx of Pleasure from the Singer of Pain" = Ada Leverson.** Editors' footnote (printed ~981, mirror): her copy of *The Ballad of Reading Gaol* (1898), now at Princeton, so inscribed; the ann.-1159 highlight is the footnote text, not a letter.
- **"Rowland, the architect of the moon" = John Rowland Fothergill (1876–1957).** Potted bio in footnote at printed ~914 (mirror): Slade, friend of Ross/Rothenstein, then London School of Architecture student; his *Ballad* copy inscribed "the Architect of the Moon"; later archaeologist with his American friend [E. P. Warren]. Fothergill index: 913, 1075 ("Architect of the Moon"), 1177.
- **Ann. 1228 + 1229 are ONE letter to George Ives**, printed 1044 (~March 1898, Paris): "Your charming friend came to see me one morning at the hotel" + the Criminal-Law-Amendment-Act repeal passage. Ives index locators: 573, 592, 619(–21), 662, 980, 1044, 1172(+). His potted-bio footnote (printed ~573n) and the Review-of-Reviews-attack footnote (printed 620–21n: "attacked Ives for an article in the Humanitarian") are both in the mirror — directly relevant to the owner's current Ives/New-Hedonism research.
- **Charlie Hickey**: bio footnote printed 649n (mirror) quoting Douglas's *Autobiography*: "young Charlie Hickey (a charming boy about a couple of years younger than myself and well known to Oscar, a son of Colonel Hickey)". Index: 649n, 811, 830, 837, 878, 1122, 1132.
- **Mellor arc, true printed pages** (index): bio 1112n; meets W at Napoule 1112; sacked from Harrow 1116; takes W to Nice 1116–18; to visit London 1121; loses bet 1126, 1131; W stays at Gland 1127–39; ancient misers 1130; W explains dislike 1132, 1138; insanity in family 1134; tedious/unbearable 1135; Swiss wine/beer 1134, 1137; too repulsive 1137; W turns against 1140; invites W to Italy 1177; Sicily/Naples/Rome 1178–87; movements in 1900, 1190n; last visit 1194–95; (post-mortem: paid W's Italy expenses, Ross letter ~printed 1227).
- **Wilkinson arc, true printed pages** (index): invents dramatic society 1109–10n (his potted-bio/backstory footnote); envied for going to Oxford 1113 (= ann. 1306 first letter); correspondence described 1118 ("Radley schoolboy… photograph most beautiful"); thanked for photographs 1122; Radley 1127 (Ross told to visit = ann. 1321), further inventions 1127n; Shakespeare's sonnets discussed 1133; thanks for *Importance* 1136 (= ann. 1328 "My dear Boy"); *Ideal Husband* copy 1159n, 1168; invited to Paris 1170–71; warned against Langrel Harris 1171; in Brussels (cont.).
- **Pollitt true printed pages** (index): 1103, 1106–7, 1115, 1120 — i.e. the annotated Pollitt letters (ann. 1308, 1313) sit at ~1115 and 1120; earlier locators are unannotated mentions.
- **Eolo (boy)**: printed 1128, 1194. **Edoardo Rolla (boy)**: printed 1140. **Maurice Gilbert** (index, not in Export A): "sends regards to Smithers 1025; beats W at bezique 1030, 1034; grows dearer to W daily 1031; signs copies for W 1032; sweeter than ever 1034; referred to by Ross as the Mauritius (cont.)" — warm material exists in CL beyond what the owner annotated.
- **Export B ↔ CL cross-anchors observed in mirror:** B-3463 ("bring the architect of the moon") = printed ~914; B-3469 ("Reggie gave me three days, and Rowland a sextette of suns") is the famous autumn-1897 to-Ross letter (CL ~printed 980 region); B-3480 ("you tried your best to create a possible life for me…") = printed ~980; B-3481 (Ives's poems "without an inscription") = printed ~980.
