# The book's own indexes, transcribed

*Merlin Holland & Rupert Hart-Davis, eds.,* The Complete Letters of Oscar Wilde *(Fourth Estate,
2000).* Two indexes, listed on the volume's contents page:

| | printed | repo PDF |
|---|---|---|
| **Index of Recipients** | 1231–1236 | 1276–1281 |
| **General Index** | 1237– | 1282–1315 |

**Why this file exists.** The indexes are set in two columns with long entries wrapping onto
indented, number-only continuation lines. The OCR mirror strips the indentation and interleaves the
columns, so a parser cannot attach continuations to the right entry — measured against printed
1232, Clifton's 7 letters parse as 5 and Douglas's 33 parse as 19, six of them stolen from Godwin
in the facing column. **So these pages are transcribed by eye from rendered page images.**

**All numbers below are PRINTED FOLIOS, not repo PDF pages.** Add roughly +45 in the 1000s, +22 in
the 400s, +25 in the 20s–60s; the offset drifts, so confirm every folio on its own page image
before citing it.

**The index is not infallible.** Its `Georges (boy)` entry includes 1097, which belongs to Monsieur
Georges the hotelier, who has his own entry. Treat it as the best available spine, not as truth.
